We are at [Saboten Con 2026](https://www.sabotencon.com) soon!
You can find us in "The Parlor" at the Renaissance Hotel, where we are sharing the room with the Go and Shogi clubs.

Come join us if you want to learn how to play Riichi mahjong, or find a match with your fellow jongers!

You can find our events on the schedule by searching for "The Parlor" (for example, the [Friday event](https://www.sabotencon.com/panels/program/the-parlor-featuring-mahjong-shogi-go?seasonId=panel-season-8db9bffc)). We aim to have teachers available throughout the event, so feel free to drop by even if you've never played before.

**Directions**: 

*From the main entrance*: go straight past the registration, take the escalators on the right up toward the "Salon" (second floor). Continue down the hallway till you see the parlors on the right.

*From the "Events Tower" entrance: take the elevator on the left, go to the "Salon" (second floor). Continue down the hallway till you see the parlors on the right.
