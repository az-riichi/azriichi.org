We will be at [Saboten Con 2026](https://www.sabotencon.com) soon!
You can find us in "The Parlor" at the Renaissance Hotel, where we'll be sharing the room with the Go and Shogi clubs.

Come join us if you want to learn how to play Riichi mahjong, or find a match with your fellow jongers!

You can find our events on the schedule by searching for "The Parlor" (for example, the [Friday event](https://www.sabotencon.com/panels/program/the-parlor-featuring-mahjong-shogi-go?seasonId=panel-season-8db9bffc)). We aim to have teachers available throughout the event, so feel free to drop by even if you've never played before.

More information to come. Stay tuned!
